# Target Journal Decision

## First Target

**Signal, Image and Video Processing (SIVP, Springer Nature)**

- Publishing model: Hybrid. The intended route is the standard subscription publication option; do not select Open Choice unless the authors later explicitly approve an APC.
- Indexing displayed by the journal: SCIE and EI Compendex.
- Scope fit: the journal focuses on practical solutions in signal, image and video processing and engineering/science applications. RA-RepDet is framed as a lightweight, reliability-aware RGB-thermal-event UAV vehicle detector with missing-modality robustness and a leakage-aware evaluation protocol.
- Speed signal: the journal displays a median submission-to-first-decision time of 4 days. This is an editorial-process metric, not a promise of acceptance or peer-review completion.

## Why SIVP Rather Than the Other Screened Options

1. The paper is an engineering-oriented multi-sensor perception study, not a broad theoretical vision contribution. SIVP’s stated emphasis on practical solutions is aligned with the paper’s clean-split reproducibility, robustness, and deployment-oriented evidence.
2. SIVP provides a materially faster first-decision signal than the fallback Machine Vision and Applications (MVA): SIVP displays 4 days versus MVA’s displayed 30 days.
3. MVA remains the preferred fallback after a scope/novelty-related SIVP rejection because its official scope explicitly includes multidimensional and multisensor machine vision and real-time engineering applications.

## Verified Submission Constraints for SIVP

- Use the Springer Nature LaTeX template and the `sn-jnl` document class; journal guidance recommends the `[iicol]` option.
- The final two-column article should be no longer than 10 pages including text, figures, and references; the tenth page may contain references only.
- Abstract length: 150–250 words.
- Provide 4–6 keywords.
- Use numbered citations in square brackets and include DOI links where available.
- Supply a PDF and editable LaTeX source files.
- Include a Data Availability Statement and declarations, including competing interests and author-contribution information submitted through the submission interface.
- Submission review is single-blind, so author names and affiliations appear in the manuscript.

## Submission Positioning

Use the following contribution framing:

> A lightweight, reproducible RGB-thermal-event UAV vehicle detector that combines reliability-aware fusion with modality-dropout training and reports all headline results on a leakage-aware blocked split with a guard band.

Do not frame the work as a general new state-of-the-art detector. Do not claim statistical significance from two seeds. Do not characterize YOLO11n as a matched architecture baseline; it is an RGB-only external baseline.

## Fallback Route

1. SIVP first.
2. MVA second if SIVP rejects on scope/novelty rather than a correctness issue.
3. Reassess only if SIVP/MVA reviews identify a concrete evidence or framing gap.
