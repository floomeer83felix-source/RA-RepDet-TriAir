# Limitations

- Accuracy is reported on a component-disjoint development-validation split that participates in checkpoint retention, not an independent test set.
- Synthetic modality removal and controlled corruption are computational interventions, not measurements of physical sensor failure.
- Dynamic routing weights are task-driven fusion coefficients and are not calibrated probabilities of sensor health or reliability.
- A positive isolated contribution from the event stream is not established by the current controls.
- No same-protocol published comparator result, external generalization result, state-of-the-art claim, or statistical-significance claim is provided.
- Checkpoints and raw data are excluded because of artifact size and dataset-rights boundaries.
