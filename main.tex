\title[RA-RepDet: Reliability-Aware RGB--Thermal--Event Fusion]{RA-RepDet: Reliability-Aware RGB--Thermal--Event Fusion for Lightweight UAV Vehicle Detection}

\author*[1]{\fnm{Nan} \sur{Xin}}\email{floomeer83felix@gmail.com}
\author[1]{\fnm{Xueting} \sur{Jin}}

\affil*[1]{\orgname{Anhui Police College}, \orgaddress{\city{Hefei}, \state{Anhui}, \country{China}}}

\abstract{Lightweight UAV vehicle detection can benefit from complementary RGB, thermal, and event cues, but evaluation can be optimistic when visually related samples cross training and validation partitions. This paper presents RA-RepDet, a compact RepViT--FPN--FCOS detector with modality-specific stems and sample-dependent softmax fusion. Evaluation uses a frozen component-disjoint development-validation protocol on TriAir and a locked 837-image within-dataset holdout accessed only after the six main checkpoints were fixed. Across three paired seeds, the preselected full configuration with modality dropout $p=0.15$ improves canonical COCO AP50:95 over matched early fusion by $0.0354\pm0.0206$ on development-validation and by $0.0062\pm0.0187$ on the locked holdout; the holdout result is mixed across seeds, although AP50 improves in all three pairs. Three-seed development-validation ablations show that dynamic gating exceeds equal-weight stem fusion by $0.0621\pm0.0188$ AP50:95 and a deterministic learned-projection control by $0.0404\pm0.0074$. Adding $p=0.15$ modality dropout within the reliability-aware architecture changes AP50:95 by $-0.0095\pm0.0258$, so dropout is not interpreted as universally beneficial. On an RTX 3090, the full front end adds 1684 parameters and 0.27 ms mean batch-one latency, while peak allocated memory increases from 122.49 to 236.40 MiB. The evidence is descriptive and within TriAir; it does not establish external generalization, calibrated sensor reliability, or real sensor-fault robustness.}

\keywords{UAV vehicle detection, RGB--thermal--event fusion, adaptive modality weighting, COCO-style evaluation, causal ablation, leakage-aware validation}
