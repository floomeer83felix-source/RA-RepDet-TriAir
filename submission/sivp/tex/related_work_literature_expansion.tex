\section{Expanded Literature Positioning}
\label{app:literature-positioning}

This draft appendix records the expanded literature base used to position RA-RepDet. It distinguishes formal peer-reviewed journal articles from leading computer-vision and machine-learning conference papers. Journal quartiles depend on the reporting year and subject category used by the submitting institution; the final author team should therefore verify the applicable JCR or SJR rule before formal submission.

\subsection{Detection, UAV, and Remote-Sensing Context}

Generic object-detection surveys describe the evolution from proposal-based pipelines to dense one-stage and transformer-based detectors \cite{Liu2020GenericOD,Zhao2019ODReview,Zou2023OD20Years}. For aerial and remote-sensing images, small targets, scale changes, crowded layouts, and arbitrary orientations remain central challenges \cite{Cheng2016RSODSurvey,Zhu2017DLRemoteSensing,Ma2019DLRemoteSensing,Deng2018MultiscaleRSOD,Wu2022UAVSurvey}. These findings motivate the multi-scale FPN--FCOS detector used here, but they do not imply that RA-RepDet replaces specialized oriented-box or transformer detectors.

The top-conference lineage is represented by Faster R-CNN, SSD, YOLO, FPN, RetinaNet, and FCOS \cite{FasterRCNN2015,SSD2016,YOLO2016,FPN2017,RetinaNet2017,FCOS2019}. Deformable convolution, ATSS, DETR, Deformable DETR, and DINO illustrate later localization and assignment developments \cite{DeformableConv2017,ATSS2020,DETR2020,DeformableDETR2021,DINO2023}. DOTA, VisDrone, and UAVDT provide complementary aerial-object detection contexts \cite{DOTA2018,VisDrone2018,UAVDT2018}.

\subsection{Multi-Modal, Visible--Thermal, and Event Perception}

Multimodal learning formulations distinguish representation, alignment, fusion, translation, and co-learning as different technical problems \cite{Baltrusaitis2019MMLSurvey,Ramachandram2017DeepMultimodal}. Visible--thermal and RGB--infrared image fusion has developed both task-agnostic fusion approaches and learning-based formulations \cite{Li2013GuidedFiltering,Ma2019InfraredSurvey,Li2019DenseFuse,Ma2019FusionGAN,Zhang2020IFCNN,Li2021RFNNest,Xu2022U2Fusion}. RA-RepDet takes a narrower detection-oriented view: it learns feature-level weighting for a shared detector rather than producing a fused display image or claiming to reconstruct a missing physical modality.

Event cameras supply asynchronous measurements that complement frame-based sensors but introduce representation and alignment challenges \cite{Gallego2022EventSurvey,Mueggler2017EventDataset,Zhu2018MVSEC,Gehrig2021DSEC}. Dataset work on multispectral pedestrian detection, low-light visible--infrared perception, and drone RGB--infrared vehicle detection provides empirical context \cite{KAIST2015,LLVIP2021ICCVW,DroneVehicle2021ICCVW}. The current model uses an already constructed event channel and therefore makes no claim about raw-event temporal-window optimization or cross-sensor synchronization beyond the local sample representation.
