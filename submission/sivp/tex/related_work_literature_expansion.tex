\section{Additional Literature Positioning}
\label{app:literature-positioning}

This appendix summarizes the broader literature used to position RA-RepDet. It is included as supporting context for journal review and does not expand the experimental claims beyond the three-seed development-validation evidence reported in the main text.

\subsection{Detection, UAV, and Remote-Sensing Context}

Generic object-detection surveys describe the evolution from proposal-based pipelines to dense one-stage and transformer-based detectors \cite{Liu2020GenericOD,Zhao2019ODReview,Zou2023OD20Years}. For aerial and remote-sensing images, small targets, scale changes, crowded layouts, and arbitrary orientations remain central challenges \cite{Cheng2016RSODSurvey,Zhu2017DLRemoteSensing,Ma2019DLRemoteSensing,Deng2018MultiscaleRSOD,Wu2022UAVSurvey}. These findings motivate the multi-scale FPN--FCOS detector used here, but they do not imply that RA-RepDet replaces specialized oriented-box or transformer detectors.

The detector lineage is represented by Faster R-CNN, SSD, YOLO, FPN, RetinaNet, and FCOS \cite{FasterRCNN2015,SSD2016,YOLO2016,FPN2017,RetinaNet2017,FCOS2019}. Deformable convolution, ATSS, DETR, Deformable DETR, and DINO illustrate later localization and assignment developments \cite{DeformableConv2017,ATSS2020,DETR2020,DeformableDETR2021,DINO2023}. DOTA, VisDrone, and UAVDT provide complementary aerial-object detection contexts \cite{DOTA2018,VisDrone2018,UAVDT2018}.

\subsection{Multi-Modal, Visible--Thermal, and Event Perception}

Multimodal learning formulations distinguish representation, alignment, fusion, translation, and co-learning as different technical problems \cite{Baltrusaitis2019MMLSurvey,Ramachandram2017DeepMultimodal}. Visible--thermal and RGB--infrared image fusion has developed both task-agnostic fusion approaches and learning-based formulations \cite{Li2013GuidedFiltering,Ma2019InfraredSurvey,Li2019DenseFuse,Ma2019FusionGAN,Zhang2020IFCNN,Li2021RFNNest,Xu2022U2Fusion}. RA-RepDet takes a narrower detection-oriented view: it learns feature-level weighting for a shared detector rather than producing a fused display image or claiming to reconstruct a missing physical modality.

Event cameras supply asynchronous measurements that complement frame-based sensors but introduce representation and alignment challenges \cite{Gallego2022EventSurvey,Mueggler2017EventDataset,Zhu2018MVSEC,Gehrig2021DSEC}. Dataset work on multispectral pedestrian detection, low-light visible--infrared perception, and drone RGB--infrared vehicle detection provides empirical context \cite{KAIST2015,LLVIP2021ICCVW,DroneVehicle2021ICCVW}. The current model uses an already constructed event channel and therefore makes no claim about raw-event temporal-window optimization or cross-sensor synchronization beyond the local sample representation.

\subsection{Robustness, Evaluation Integrity, and Lightweight Deployment}

The missing-modality intervention discussed in this manuscript is related to broader multimodal robustness and regularization ideas \cite{ModDrop2016,HeMIS2016,CrossModalDistillation2016,ModalityHallucination2016,Srivastava2014Dropout}. It remains a synthetic channel-zeroing experiment rather than a complete physical-sensor-failure model. The validation protocol is treated as part of the evidence: benchmark work and later analyses of selection bias and leakage motivate explicit limits on claims drawn from a fixed validation split \cite{Everingham2010VOC,Russakovsky2015ImageNet,Geiger2013KITTI,Cawley2010JMLR,Kapoor2023Patterns,Northcutt2021LabelErrors,Recht2019ImageNet}.

The lightweight design is informed by efficient CNN, re-parameterization, compression, and vision-transformer literature \cite{Krizhevsky2017CACM,Khan2020CNN,Sze2017EfficientDNN,Cheng2018CompressionSurvey,MobileNetV2_2018,EfficientNet2019,MobileNetV3_2019,RepVGG2021,RepViT2024CVPR}. Vision-transformer surveys and representative architectures provide a broader comparison point for future extensions, while the current submission retains RepViT as the tested backbone \cite{Khan2022TransformersVision,Han2023VisionTransformer,ViT2021,Swin2021}. Data augmentation and broad deep-learning reviews are cited only for background; no unreported augmentation or compression experiment is claimed \cite{Shorten2019Augmentation,Alzubaidi2021DLReview,Minaee2022SegmentationSurvey,Voulodimos2018DLVision}.

\subsection{Scope of the Appendix}

The appendix is intended to broaden the literature context for journal review. It should not be read as evidence that every cited work uses the same sensor suite, data split, or evaluation protocol as RA-RepDet, nor as a claim that the present validation-only evidence resolves the broader open problems of cross-dataset generalization, real sensor degradation, or causal modality attribution.
