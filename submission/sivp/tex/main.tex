\abstract{Lightweight multi-sensor UAV vehicle detection must balance complementary RGB, thermal, and event cues with an evaluation protocol that does not overstate performance through visual overlap. We present RA-RepDet, a RepViT--FPN--FCOS detector for five-channel RGB--thermal--event samples. The proposed R4 configuration combines modality-specific stems, learned softmax reliability-aware fusion, and training-time modality dropout with $p=0.20$. Headline evidence is reported on a frozen leakage-aware block64/guard16 split with 7439 training images, 2213 validation images, and 837 guard images; integrity checks found zero exact RGB train/validation matches and zero same-family guard-band violations. In controlled runs with seeds 0 and 2, R4 achieved mean AP50 of 0.962495, AP75 of 0.891266, and $F1@0.50$ of 0.920861. R4 outperformed the matched tri-modal early-fusion baseline and retained AP50 values of 0.916051, 0.718277, and 0.961577 under synthetic removal of RGB, thermal, and event inputs, respectively. An RGB-only YOLO11n result is reported as an external reference rather than a matched architecture ablation. The conclusions are limited to validation evidence from two controlled seeds, one dataset, and synthetic missingness; thermal removal remains the most difficult evaluated condition.}
