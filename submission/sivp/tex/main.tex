\title[RA-RepDet: Reliability-Aware RGB--Thermal--Event Fusion]{RA-RepDet: Reliability-Aware RGB--Thermal--Event Fusion for Lightweight UAV Vehicle Detection}

\author*[1]{\fnm{Nan} \sur{Xin}}\email{floomeer83felix@gmail.com}
\author[1]{\fnm{Xueting} \sur{Jin}}

\affil*[1]{\orgname{Anhui Police College}, \orgaddress{\city{Hefei}, \state{Anhui}, \country{China}}}

\abstract{Lightweight UAV vehicle detection can benefit from complementary RGB, thermal, and event cues, but results may be optimistic when visually related samples cross training and evaluation partitions. This paper presents RA-RepDet, a compact RepViT--FPN--FCOS detector comparing matched early fusion with a reliability-aware front end using modality-specific stems, softmax fusion, and modality-dropout training. Evaluation uses a frozen component-disjoint development-validation protocol on the publicly available TriAir dataset and a subsequent locked same-dataset held-out check. Across three paired development-validation seeds, reliability-aware fusion with modality dropout $p=0.15$ achieves descriptive mean gains of +0.0185 F1, +0.0161 AP50, and +0.0647 AP75 over matched early fusion using project-local AP metrics. A locked 837-image holdout evaluation with the same fixed checkpoints shows smaller mean gains of +0.0069 F1, +0.0086 AP50, and +0.0022 AP75, with mixed per-seed F1 and AP75 deltas. The study supports a bounded within-dataset assessment of reliability-aware tri-modal fusion while explicitly avoiding claims of external-dataset generalization, COCO AP50:95 performance, or physical sensor-failure robustness.}

\keywords{UAV vehicle detection, RGB--thermal--event fusion, reliability-aware fusion, RepViT--FCOS, component-disjoint validation, locked holdout evaluation}
